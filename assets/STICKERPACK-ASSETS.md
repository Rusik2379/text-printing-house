Hero: исходник пользователя `сайт/Баннера/!стикерпаки.png`. Встроенный image_gen удалил фон, розовые мазки, красные декоративные отметки и рукописную надпись; робот и наборы сохранены. Итог: `stickerpack-robot-transparent.png`, WebP-копия с alpha.

Keep ONLY the original robot and all the stickerpacks, sheet stacks, loose star and heart in front of it, unchanged in appearance, colors, fine detail and composition. Preserve the exact Russian lettering printed on the stickerpack packaging and the GAMER sticker. Do not redraw, replace or crop the robot or products. Entire subject fully visible. Output actual clean RGBA alpha transparency around the robot and products, including gaps between them. No replacement background, no checkerboard painted into image, no new text.»
